# Cat City Rampage

Status: work in progress. It is in the repo but not on the Kidzone shelf yet.

Two mischievous cats race up a skyscraper, smashing and clawing their way to the top to grab the yarn ball.

## How to Play

**Player 1 (Red Cat):**
- `W` `A` `D` to move and jump
- `Space` to destroy bricks

**Player 2 (Blue Cat):**
- `↑` `←` `→` to move and jump
- `Enter` to destroy bricks

**On Touch:**
- Use each cat's buttons to move, jump, and smash bricks

First cat to reach the yarn at the top of the building wins!

## Game Loop

1. Both cats start at the bottom of the building
2. Move up by jumping and destroying bricks to clear a path
3. Destroyed bricks stay gone (create shortcuts!)
4. Reach the glowing yarn ball at the top to win
5. No timers, no losing—just pure rampage chaos

## Core Features

- Two-player local competitive play
- Physics-based movement with gravity and jumping
- Destructible building bricks
- Touch and keyboard controls
- Instant reset/play again

## Assets

All graphics are hand-coded (no external images):
- Cats are simple shapes with eyes
- Bricks are tiled rectangles with borders
- Yarn is a glowing yellow circle at the top
- Sky gradient background
